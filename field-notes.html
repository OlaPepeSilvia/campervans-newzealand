The dream itinerary

Auckland to Queenstown. Two weeks, two-thousand-and-change kilometres, and a landscape that one-ups itself the whole way south. You’ll start with volcanoes and harbour views in the City of Sails and end with glacier lakes and mountain skylines in the adventure capital of the world.

This guide is built from the pins our crew and travellers have dropped over years of running this route. The overnights anchor the days; the pins fill them. Fourteen days is the sweet spot: enough to give the Coromandel, Rotorua, and the South Island a proper crack without ever feeling like you’re just transiting.

Day 1

Auckland

🚚 Pick-up day · 0 km🛏 Overnight: Auckland
Auckland harbour / Waitemata

Pick up the van, do the supermarket run, and spend the rest of the day on foot. Auckland is the easiest big city in New Zealand to enjoy in an afternoon: park up near the waterfront and everything below is within a few kilometres.

Things to do

  • Waitemata Harbour & Wynyard Quarter — a stroll along the waterfront with the Harbour Bridge framing every photo.
  • Sky Tower — walk it at golden hour, or jump off it if you’re feeling brave.
  • Rangitoto Island — the world’s youngest shield volcano, a 25-minute ferry ride from downtown.

Food & drink

  • Eighthirty Coffee Roasters — morning caffeine, CBD.
  • Ortolana — post-walk dinner in Britomart.
  • Caretaker — cocktails in a basement bar before an early night.
Dunc’s tip: Got a spare day either side? Waiheke Island and Tiritiri Matangi are both world-class and criminally skipped. The Waitakere Ranges and Piha Beach are an easy West Coast detour too.

Day 2

Coromandel Peninsula

🚚 ~120 km🛏 Overnight: Hahei or Hot Water Beach
Cathedral Cove / Coromandel coastline

Two hours east of Auckland the pohutukawa-lined coast of the Coromandel begins, and the day turns into a beach crawl. The shortest driving day of the trip on purpose: the peninsula rewards slow.

Things to do

  • Cathedral Cove — a proper walk through the bush to a sea arch that earns its reputation. Go early to beat the crowds.
  • Hot Water Beach — bring a spade, dig a pool in the sand two hours either side of low tide, and sit in your own private hot spring.

Free camp

  • Hahei Holiday Resort — powered sites, 10-minute walk to Cathedral Cove trailhead. Book ahead in summer.
  • Hot Water Beach Top 10 — steps from the beach, friendly staff, great facilities.

Day 3

Rotorua

🚚 ~170 km🛏 Overnight: Rotorua
Wai-O-Tapu / Champagne Pool

If it’s a Wednesday or Saturday, start at the Rotorua Night Market on the way in. Then give Rotorua what it deserves: a full day. The geothermal parks are the single best paid activity in the central North Island.

Things to do

  • Wai-O-Tapu Thermal Wonderland — Champagne Pool, Lady Knox Geyser, and the Artist’s Palette. Allow two hours minimum.
  • Redwoods Treewalk — suspended bridges through a cathedral grove of giant California redwoods. Magical at dusk when the lanterns come on.

Free camp

  • Blue Lake Holiday Park — 10 minutes from the city, on the shore of Lake Tikitapu. Arrive before 4pm in summer.
