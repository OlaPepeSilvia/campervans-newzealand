The dream itinerary

Brisbane to Cairns. Two weeks, two thousand-and-change kilometres, and a coastline that keeps one-upping itself the whole way north. You’ll start with rooftop bars and koalas in the river city and end with reef islands and rainforest waterfalls in the tropics.

This guide is built from the pins our crew and travellers have dropped over years of running this route. The overnights anchor the days; the pins fill them. Fourteen days is the sweet spot: enough to give K’gari, the Whitsundays and the Cassowary Coast a proper crack without ever feeling like you’re just transiting.

Day 1

Brisbane

🚚 Pick-up day · 0 km🛏 Overnight: Brisbane
Brisbane skyline / South Bank

Pick up the van, do the supermarket run, and spend the rest of the day on foot. Brisbane is the easiest big city in Australia to enjoy in an afternoon: park up near South Bank and everything below is within a few kilometres.

Things to do

  • South Bank Parklands & Streets Beach — an actual man-made beach in the middle of the city. Free, lifeguarded, ridiculous in the best way.
  • Story Bridge — walk it at golden hour, or climb it if you’re feeling spendy.
  • Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary — the world’s oldest koala sanctuary, 20 minutes from the city.

Food & drink

  • Coffee Anthology — morning caffeine, CBD.
  • Southbank Beer Garden — post-walk beers by the river.
  • Sixteen Antlers Rooftop Bar — sunset over the city before an early night.
Dunc’s tip: Got a spare day either side? North Stradbroke and Moreton Island are both world-class and criminally skipped. Tamborine Mountain’s falls are an easy hinterland detour too.

Day 2

Glass House Mountains & the hinterland

🚚 ~110 km🛏 Overnight: Sunshine Coast hinterland
Glass House Mountains / Kondalilla Falls

An hour north of Brisbane the volcanic plugs of the Glass House Mountains rise out of the pineapple farms, and the day turns into a waterfall crawl. The shortest driving day of the trip on purpose: the hinterland rewards slow.

Things to do

  • Mt Tibrogargan summit — a proper scramble, not a stroll. Sensational views if you’re confident on rock.
  • Mount Beerwah — the big one of the group; base walk if the summit’s beyond you.

Waterfalls & swimming holes

  • Gardners Falls — rope swing, easy access, Maleny.
  • Kondalilla Falls — the hinterland headliner: 90m falls and a rock pool at the top.
  • Coomba Falls — a longer inland detour, deep green swimming hole, worth it on a hot day.

Day 3

Noosa

🚚 ~70 km🛏 Overnight: Noosa / Six Mile Creek
Noosa National Park / Tea Tree Bay

If it’s a Wednesday or Saturday, start at the Eumundi Markets on the way in. Then give Noosa what it deserves: a full day. The national park coastal walk is the single best free activity on the Sunshine Coast.

Things to do

  • Noosa National Park coastal walk — Tea Tree Bay, Fairy Pools and Hell’s Gates. Keep an eye out for koalas and dolphins.
  • Mount Tinbeerwah — ten-minute walk, hundred-kilometre view. Sunset spot.

Free camp

  • Six Mile Creek Rest Area — 20 minutes inland, free, popular. Arrive before 4pm.
